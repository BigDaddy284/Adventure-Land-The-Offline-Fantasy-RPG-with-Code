WHAT'S HAPPENING IN YOUR WORLD
Events

Fantasy Land is one world running on your own PC, so there are no realms, home servers or server hopping to worry about. Events come in three kinds: holidays you switch on yourself, scheduled events that follow the game's clock, and rare creatures that turn up on their own.

HolidaysHoliday Season, Lunar New Year, Valentine's, Halloween and Egg Hunt. Switch any of them on or off while you play with the candy cane button above the skill bar.
Event monstersGrinch, Snowman, Mr Pumpkin, Mr Green, Slenderman, Dragold, Love Goo and Wabbit each have their own switch in the same menu. They spawn even when their holiday is off.
Scheduled eventsEvery day at 13:00 and 20:00 UTC, Goo Brawl, Giga Crab or A/B Testing starts, taking turns. At 23:00 UTC it's Ice Golem or Franky. The game has to be running at the time.
Rare creaturesGolden bats, cute bees, mimics and other rare monsters appear on their own as you play.

Joining an event
Event buttonWhile a scheduled event is running, an EVENT button can appear in game. Click it to join.
TravelEvent bosses and holiday monsters live on the map. Walk there, or use smart_move in CODE.
NPCsSome holidays add an NPC with a shop or an exchange.
Your settingsDrop luck, gold and the other Mod Menu settings apply to event drops too.
Bean's home server and Favoré's server blessings come from the online game. With only one world they don't change anything, so you can ignore them.
Event guides

Holiday Season, Lunar New Year, Valentine's, Halloween, Egg Hunt, Goo Brawl, Giga Crab, A/B Testing, Ice Golem, Franky.
