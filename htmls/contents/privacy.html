This privacy policy explains how your data is used and stored by {{ domain.name }}.

Everything stays on your own PC. Your account, characters, items and settings are kept in the game's local database inside the game folder, and nothing is sent to anyone.

There are no analytics, no tracking and no payments. The built-in account is only reachable from this PC, and its email address isn't real.

Cookies are only used to keep you logged in and to remember your settings in the game window.

The game never updates itself. Apart from fetching the parts it needs on the very first start, it never goes online by itself. The only other time anything opens online is if you click a link yourself, such as the link to the source code.
